From booking to your mat

Four short steps. Book one to four weeks ahead.

  1. Step 1

    Pick your time

    Choose an open session on the calendar.

  2. Step 2

    Sign the waiver

    A quick form with your details and an online signature.

  3. Step 3

    Send your deposit

    50% by e-Transfer within 24 hours confirms your spot.

  4. Step 4

    Tell us about you

    A short questionnaire about your goals, sent once you're confirmed.

Cancellations: 24 hours' notice moves your deposit to a new date.

Late arrivals: sessions end on time. After 15 minutes without word, it counts as missed.

Events: a 50% deposit holds your date, with the balance due 7 days before.
